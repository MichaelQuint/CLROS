# Step 1328 - Continuing TCP scheduler witness implementation

## Summary

Add one fixed cooperative worker to the default-off M07-026 network profile.
The worker increments owned progress once per dispatch, yields through the
accepted context/lifecycle infrastructure and later returns exit value 17.
Fresh supervisor confirmation precedes reap, guard restoration and pool scrub.
This is development implementation; the eight networking issues remain open.

## Why

Read-only review found that the existing finite TCP scenario did not prove
#152's literal no-scheduler-starvation acceptance criterion. Bounded nonblocking
retransmission and other connections progressing do not establish runnable-task
progress. Preserve that criterion and implement its missing witness.

## Evidence

`scheduler-witness-`. The worker uses the accepted pool/context/stack layout and
one actual lifecycle slot. Packet and final TIME-WAIT dispatches are separately
counted. A dispatch advances progress exactly once; initial, intervening and final
observed gaps must be at most one second. The next dispatch is due after 100 ms.

These are finite reviewed paths, not general unwind or interrupt bounds.
