## Summary

Corrects qualification checks after the frozen step1135 implementation. The
kernel/EFI production sources are unchanged. Full-host acceptance is pending.

## Why

Independent review found a contradictory-record gap: the interrupt transcript
required one acquire but the reused polling transcript allowed a larger count.
The full-host fast phase also exposed old boot-surface declarations, duplicated
first-exit dependent-profile lists and an outdated central entry-source hash.

## Files changed

- `QemuTranscripts.VirtqueueInterrupt.cs` now binds queue acquire/fence counts
  to the interrupt records. `M05VirtqueueInterruptEfiIntegrationTests.cs` mutates
  only the queue acquire count to two and requires rejection.
- `EfiContracts.cs` owns `FirstExitDependentProbes`, consumed by the M03 typed
  exit, M04 reference-barrier, module-registration and GC-root source guards.
  Full per-profile runtime rejection loops remain intact. Its normalized
  `EfiEntrySourceSha256` is refreshed to the reviewed step1135 entry source.
- `BootSequencePlanTests.cs` lists the two new guarded methods and the two new
  default-off probe declaration pairs. No host-only surface is admitted.
