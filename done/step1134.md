## Evidence

MADT malformed/duplicate/topology/address/record-limit controls preserve input
and default outputs. APIC tests verify identity and feature admission, every
callback failure, complete PIC/IOAPIC/LVT masking, stopped timer, TPR/SVR
readback and rejection of existing in-service/high-priority pending vectors.
MSI-X tests cover capability-chain controls, full table/PBA ranges, BAR pairs,
overlap, 1/65/2048-entry programming, stale pending bits and every prepare access
failure. Queue tests verify handler-before-acquire ordering, no polling fallback,
mask-before-reset, failed-mask retention and the existing polling behavior.

`artifacts/backlog-current/apic-leaf-review-r01` records independent LLVM
disassembly of original emitted bytes. The observation leaf preserves RBX and
checks CPL/IF and CPU features before RDMSR. The wait leaf preserves R15, loads
a temporary IDT only inside its bounded region and restores IF=0/original IDTR.
The returning handler preserves RAX and captures RIP/RFLAGS and ISR7 before/after
EOI; the spurious handler does not acknowledge EOI. Exact byte mutation and
context-layout tests pass. This is byte review, not execution or final PE proof.

Primary register/ABI references: Intel SDM Vol3A June 2024 revision084, chapter11
and 64-bit interrupt-frame rules
(`https://cdrdv2-public.intel.com/825758/253668-sdm-vol-3a.pdf`); Intel 82093AA
section3 (`https://cs.nyu.edu/~mwalfish/classes/ut/s10-cs372h/ref/ia32/ioapic.pdf`);
packed ACPI declarations in TianoCore `Acpi65.h`; MSI-X register layouts in the
Linux primary `include/uapi/linux/pci_regs.h`. No reference source was copied.
