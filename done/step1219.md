## Why

before IF can be enabled. The control layer tracks partial writes, exact
ISR/EOI/reload ordering and retained deadline/critical-phase state. It borrows
the APIC callbacks and reuses DeadlineQueue without adding a second queue or
reinterpreting #186's cooperative IRQ-off contexts as interrupt frames.
