## Summary

Develop owned #136–#140 with shared #633. Connect step1158's modern PCI binding
and VERSION_1-only policy to one default-off owned EFI child after original M05

## Why

Virtio1.2 section5.1.5 requires receive buffers during initialization. Both
size-eight rings and fixed buffers fit in one owned page, preserving the accepted
allocator restore path. A used entry alone does not establish wire bytes; QEMU
filter-dump and independent compiled libpcap checks provide that qualification.
Primary references: https://docs.oasis-open.org/virtio/virtio/v1.2/virtio-v1.2.html
and https://www.qemu.org/docs/master/system/qemu-manpage.html.
