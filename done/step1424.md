  owner/epoch/generation/borrower leases span pending, established and fd-less

  copied/stale leases cannot issue, release, replace or erase another lifetime.
- First real-clock borrow starts240 seconds of quiet time, including startup
  with lost history. Secret replacement needs a drained owner, greater epoch
  and newly acquired unequal key, then full quiet time. No live rotation or
  clock/counter entropy fallback. Unequal bytes do not establish source freshness.
- Receive ignores new SYNs during quiet time and retains full existing
  TIME-WAIT/duplicate-FIN protection. Sequence storage is excluded from network
  extents/frames/outputs and thread attachment/wait metadata. End releases only
  after all transport storage drains. Finite diagnostic ISN behavior is unchanged.
- A third, independent pre-exit firmware acquisition supplies the TCP key;
  AT_RANDOM and service getrandom retain distinct acquisitions/owners. All capture
  payloads and source callbacks are cleared on success/failure. Worker or network
  borrowers block cleanup; drained cleanup verifies complete owner erasure.
  Serial reports source/lease/count facts only. The native leaf has no post-exit
  fresh-key provider; explicit trusted reseed is a host-tested contract.

## Why

The finite diagnostic `NextSequence +=65536` permanently rejects new SYNs after
its admitted32-bit boundary, even after every slot returns. Removing its guard
would leave predictable sequence allocation. Continuing admission now uses an
independent secret/tuple/time policy while preserving finite profile evidence.
Primary references: [RFC9293 section3.4.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9293.html#section-3.4.1)
and [RFC6528 section3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6528.html#section-3).
The implementation is original C#; no RFC code was imported.
