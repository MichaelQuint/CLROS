# Step 1020 - Build retained identity mappings and exclude owned stack guards

## What changed and why

Issue #64 needs a retained image larger than the existing page-fault diagnostic's
eight short ranges. Add separately named `BuildRetainedImage` and
`AuditRetainedImage` entry points to the existing builder, reusing its encoder,
hierarchy walk and page-zero audit. Legacy entry points keep their 8-range,
16-page limits and conservative table bound.

The retained profile accepts 1..32 disjoint ranges, up to 4096 pages per range
and 8192 caller pages total, plus the explicit table arena. It admits WB RO/NX,
RX and RW/NX pages. Before output writes, count distinct 2-MiB, 1-GiB and
512-GiB prefixes, including the arena's self-mapping, to obtain the exact table
requirement. Keep the 55-page table limit and reject insufficient capacity
without modifying metadata or entries. No allocation or second encoder added.

`FirstExitOwnedArena.WriteMappingRanges` uses the arena's existing guard
predicate to produce five RW/NX intervals. The prepared hierarchy maps its 97
retained pages and omits all eight guards. Invalid capacity, overlapping output
or damaged canaries refuse before range writes. This is preparation, not a
live guard-page or permanent-CR3 claim.
