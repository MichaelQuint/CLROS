## Changes

- Add `apps/NativeAotSocketContainment/Program.cs` and its C#14/.NET11 project.
  The accepted NativeAotSocketSmoke source, original ELF and source authority
  remain unchanged. Unique initial bytes0/1/2 assign three nonblocking sockets
  independent of accept order. Role0 queues at least32768 checked bytes until
  actual WouldBlock, then requires one TimedOut after at least60 monotonic seconds,
  subsequent EOF and Shutdown on send. Roles1/2 exchange fifteen checked bytes
  over at least70 seconds and finish after the timeout and independent worker
  progress. Join the worker before PASS; the complete case has a100-second limit.
  Main polling is100ms and worker sleep250ms to respect the existing finite
 4096-call-per-thread budget. Actual guest budget consumption is still unmeasured.
- Add `tools/OS.QemuRunner/SocketContainmentPeer.cs`. Learn actual guest sequence
  numbers, withhold all role0 data ACKs, check deterministic payload/retries and
  exchange healthy bytes every five seconds. Require six head transmissions
  spanning at least30 seconds,30 healthy responses and one VLAN injection.
  Schedule later rounds from the first completed exchange, so role assignment
  cannot shorten the required70-second healthy interval. Earlier peer ACKs and
  duplicate SYN-ACKs remain valid during subsequent handshakes. Both healthy
  peers receive exact-sequence resets at the end; ordinary close/TIME-WAIT
  remains covered by its existing ancestors. Completion and joined cleanup are
  mandatory; the peer cannot confer guest acceptance.
- Add explicit `--socket-containment-peer` to the direct OS.QemuRunner CLI and
  command builder. Require the captured dedicated modern ARP network fixture,
 120..295-second timeout and ordinary diagnostic exit. Reject duplicate modes,
  usernet forwarding and other peer/HTTP/panic combinations. No listener or
  capture is created by dry-run. The high-level Tooling qualification/profile,
  new original payload admission and final evidence consumer remain pending.
- Extend `QemuEthernetPeer.cs` only through an explicit tagged diagnostic:
  admit exactly1518-byte802.1Q frames while ordinary sessions retain1514.
  Invalid outgoing sizes/types consume no prefix; invalid incoming extended
  content retires the transport after reading its bounded frame.
- In `src/OS/Boot/EfiEntry.LinuxSockets.cs`, emit `M07_027_TIMEOUT` only after
  observing the exact timed-out connection closed/reset with shared Failed0.
  Emit `M07_028_DROP` only after actual device drop/repost progress with the
  device still running; record before/after counts and available indexes.
  Observations do not change packets, timers, ring completions or scheduling.
  Refresh only this file's CRLF-to-LF source identity in EfiContracts.Sources.cs.
- Add `tests/OS.HostTests/SocketContainmentPeerTests.cs`: parsed wire values,
  detached payload copies, truncation/checksum/tuple/options/padding controls,
