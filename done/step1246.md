## Why

Step1245's pipe path blocked only for readability. Capacity ownership must also
retain partial progress across a genuine Park/resume without replaying the
write or losing the tail. After waking the child's private futex, the parent
now blocks on the empty pipe. The child begins its 4,100-byte streaming write,
publishes 4,096 bytes, wakes the reader and parks for capacity. The parent reads
all 4,096 bytes, validates both end sentinels and parks on the child's clear-TID.
The child resumes its original operation, writes exactly four remaining bytes
and exits. After the actual join the parent reads and validates that tail.

The existing source spans the parent's read-only-in-practice TLS page plus four
bytes of the child's unchanged TLS value. It never overlaps the destination.
One additional static RW/NX user page at 0x406000 receives the 4,096-byte read;
the two user stacks, both TLS pages and shared control words remain disjoint.
The ELF grows from six to seven pages; package/proc policy and exact canonical
preflight follow its actual payload length. Kernel worker pool geometry stays
unchanged. The source and destination sentinels make both the full-buffer
transfer and the four-byte continuation independently observable.

The same two-thread quota, four generations, masks/FS restoration, errno
controls and final genuine HPET timeout remain required. The strict parser
admits only the exact added blocked call/completion order and actual fd/RIP
arguments; no synthetic transfer or tick is introduced into the native owner.
