## What and why

Continue owned #218/#219 within shared #737 and PR740. The exact immutable
manifest environment selector `CLROS_LOG_BUDGET_CASE=records64` selects a
64-callback process-lifetime logging budget. Absence retains 1MiB/4096 callbacks;
invalid/duplicate selectors reject before owner initialization. No app source or
ELF publication changed. Startup must complete before the control can qualify.

`LinuxAppLogQuotaObservation` joins successfully copied callbacks, actual rejected
charges and real write/writev results under the existing serialized owner. It
requires a prior complete diagnostic catalog row, exact copied/charged counters,
64 callbacks and actual EIO5. Rejected bytes never enter serial, charged state is
never refunded/reset, and unjoined/foreign/contradictory output fails permanently.
The first-EIO summary is a snapshot; later denied calls remain checked.

Descriptor `WriteVector` coalesces vectors into one scratch-buffer sink callback.
The real control caught the earlier assumption that quota could reject the LF
after delivering a 32-byte first vector. That assumption was removed: callback64
succeeds whole, and the next callback returns EIO without copied output. Forged
positive partial returns are rejected. The independent review also identified
unjoined-denial completion and short-scalar-request gaps; both are guarded.

The runner admits only `--web-log-case normal|records64` for continuing web,
rejects duplicate/unsupported/nonweb use, and records the exact case. The quota
case requires POST exercise500 with an empty body, repeat409 and later selected
health200 from the same owned QEMU. Raw serial, runner summaries and five ordered
packet streams bind to the package-selected case. Ordinary case behavior remains
200/exact logging catalog, repeat409 and later health200.
