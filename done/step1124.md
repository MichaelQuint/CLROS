# Step 1124 - Implement the bounded post-exit monotonic clock

## Summary

Development evidence for #96 (M05-003) and shared integration #608, based on
accepted ACPI selection in step1123. The default-off clock diagnostic starts
an owned HPET after firmware exit. Focused checks and the first real 83-record
QEMU capture pass. Full-host, ordinary-regression, native-stack and independent
image-association acceptance are pending; both issues remain open/claimed.

## Why

Later platform work needs an owned monotonic counter, with explicit frequency,
wrap limits and bounded handling of a halted or enabled-but-stalled firmware
timer. Startup permits one recovery; it never spins indefinitely or resets a
published clock. A declared Intel TSC fallback requires complete invariant-TSC
and CPUID.15H frequency observations; missing crystal data is not guessed.

## Evidence

`0x009896808086A201`, period 10,000,000 fs, frequency 100,000,000 Hz and 64-bit
counter. One startup recovery succeeded. Published samples increased from
21,710 to 27,450 ticks. The guest did not enumerate a usable TSC frequency.
The three rejection controls and retained canaries passed; 83 application
records were ordered and unique. Fifteen firmware escape bytes precede the
application stream and are not CLROS records.

## Failures

Initial mapping tests needed an address namespace and explicit native-size casts.
The first profile/source checks caught a misplaced no-stdlib probe guard, an
enum name interpreted as an unavailable BCL constant, and an unsorted probe
inventory. Those were corrected; the contract audit and 48-check boot fixture
pass. Early `clock-profile-r01.log`/`r02.log` and
`clock-boot-authority-r01.log` preserve failures. The freshness guard also refused
two focused runs made stale by reviewed identity updates; no stale run is evidence.

## Evidence boundary

This is implemented, partly validated development work. HPET behavior is proven
only for this QEMU/OVMF diagnostic. Host tests cover synthetic stalled-enabled
recovery, persistent stall, fallback, wrap and malformed inputs; they do not
establish live TSC behavior or arbitrary hardware compatibility. No advancement
of Gate 6, general EH, hosted .NET/Kestrel, cloud or production readiness.
