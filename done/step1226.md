## Why

The existing queues and cooperative switch need one owner for publication,
check/register/park, competing completion and delayed reclamation. Host mock
`OSHostThreadTable` is unsuitable for caller-owned kernel resources. One stable
table now owns those transitions, while its trusted native caller must supply
real IRQ serialization and mapped-lifetime/supervisor observations.
