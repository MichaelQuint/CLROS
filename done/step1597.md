## Why

lifetimes. Cached open facts alone cannot supply current chain allocation.
Metadata user output must retain its original mapping and capacity through the
same real owned waits used for disk reads. Diagnostic stdout can itself advance
a cooperative device: an exit-race witness must not consume its target event.
