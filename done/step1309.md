## Why

The accepted step1164 ARP profile resets and releases its single-exchange backing.
It cannot service continuing traffic. Keep actual ring descriptors, eight RX
buffers, bounded TX capacity and generation metadata alive across calls instead
of introducing another synthetic readiness planner or copying the one-shot owner.
One trusted IRQ-off owner must eventually connect these operations to actual
PCI/MMIO/notification/time/reset services. The ring engine alone cannot prove them.

## Files changed

- `src/OS.Kernel/Drivers/ContinuingVirtioNet.cs`: one main public type with nested
  status/state. Admission validates disjoint explicit16-entry metadata and32KiB
  backing, guards copied/retired state, and publishes eight real RX descriptors.
  TX copies/queues full Ethernet frames, rejects capacity exhaustion without
  mutation and validates all observed used entries before bounded reclamation.
  RX checks used distance/IDs/lengths/header, retries short output without
  consumption, copies into caller storage and replenishes the original buffer.
  Fatal ownership/order/protocol faults retain backing until reset-gated cleanup.
- `tests/OS.HostTests/ContinuingVirtioNetTests.cs`: nine independent wire/lifetime
  cases, including real16-bit wrap, no partial malformed-batch reclaim, pending
  capacity/partial out-of-order completion, alias/copy guards, no managed
  allocation, failed-fence retention and scrub only after quiescence.
- `docs/networking.md`: current development scope and missing live proof.
- `.gitea/backlog/clros-issues.json`: correct only the seven batch ownership
  aliases from obsolete `src/OS/EfiEntry.cs` to `src/OS/Boot/EfiEntry.cs`, plus
  #691 claim/current evidence. No dependency, acceptance or closure intent changes.

The class borrows a `DmaAddressRange`; it does not allocate/release DMA-pool pages
or manufacture reset authority. The reset receipt records a trusted adapter's
decision. Actual reset, bus-master disable and real fence/readback remain that
adapter's mandatory responsibility. Output from a failed RX repost must not be
consumed. No new EFI compile item/probe/profile or first-party native source is added.
