## Native review

683 managed COFF/EFI methods associate. The mapping closure contains 167
methods; review checked 298 returns, 15 tails, 1,013 stack stores, Win64 call
alignment and saved registers. Only the two bounded four-level walks recurse;
there are no unresolved calls. Bounds are 8,016 bytes below RunPhysicalMapping,
8,112 below Diagnose and 8,176 below the permanent continuation, within its
32,768-byte diagnostic reserve.

The enabled comparison preserves 682 normalized methods. Only the stackless
MappingExecutionReady changes from xor eax,eax; ret to mov eax,1; ret. There
are no other method-location/length, unwind, relocation-target or addend changes.
The bound therefore carries to the executed image.

The original review report mislabeled 131,072 as whole owned-stack capacity.
That number is the pre-exit reservation. The enabled comparison records the
correction without modifying the hashed original: usable owned stack is
262,120 bytes; the diagnostic reservation remains 32,768 bytes.
