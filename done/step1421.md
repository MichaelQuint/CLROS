## Why

Finite diagnostic lifetime ceilings and retained web-prefix ownership cannot
establish a continuing service. A global progress counter also cannot detect a
busy worker while peers progress. Compact TIME-WAIT must preserve real deadlines
after bulk reuse. Continuing randomness needs a reviewed construction and
separate owned seeds, rather than expansion of the finite4096-byte reservoir.
