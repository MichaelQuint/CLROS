# Step 1294 - Preserve free-page zeroing without repeated whole-pool clearing

## Change and scope

LinuxMemoryServices.Apply previously cleared every unheld page of the exclusive
pool on every successful mapping/advice mutation. Begin already zeroes the whole
pool, and every release preserves the free-zero invariant. Rewriting already-free
pages adds pool-sized work even for sparse reservations and policy-only changes.

Apply now clears fresh pages and previously backed pages that become free. The
old retained region inventory is consulted before publishing new state. Existing
contents survive permission/advice changes; fresh/reused pages and releases are
still zeroed. Candidate accounting, complete root build/audit and mutation guard
all finish before any live memory change. No admission, ownership, table, region,
backing, virtual, timer, epoch, step or time bound changes.

PAL/build docs, this receipt and scoped managed evidence fields. No application,
