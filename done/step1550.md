## Why

The exact0ad5ffd4 original still misses the required scheduling witness. Serial
records yield25456, a blocking futex and two further futex calls25609-25865,
clock25869, write25873, tgkill25878, IRQ25880, waiter readiness25936 and competing
read25960. The futexes may include native transition/GC synchronization; their
precise caller is not attributed. The existing lifetime.Check necessarily adds
the observed monotonic clock query after yield and before write.

Move clock/token preparation before the last explicit yield, preserving the
same lifetime/start/budget and checking it again immediately after the finite
transaction inside Actor's existing try/catch. A deadline or cancellation after
write cannot abandon the successfully sent signal. Finished publication,
join-before-Sent readback, acknowledgment-before-unregister and separate finite
cleanup remain. The null-lifetime legacy path is unchanged.

This changes the timestamp sampled at transaction entry. Expiry during yield
with delayed CTS delivery can allow late side effects that the post-check then
rejects. The earlier Check -> P/Invoke also permitted expiry during preemption;
neither provides atomic deadline enforcement for every side effect. The same
absolute120s success deadline is retained, without deadline refresh or a new
attempt. GC/preemption remains possible and race success is not guaranteed.
