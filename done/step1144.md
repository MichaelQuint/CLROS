# Step 1144 - Live block contract and LBA0 implementation; qualification pending

## Summary

Implement #122/#123 under claimed integration #625 after accepted step1143.
The existing IReadOnlyBlockDevice contract now has a one-attempt live adapter,
a narrow pointer-backed native Span surface, and protective MBR validation.
Focused host checks and selected no-stdlib C# compilation pass. Native lowering,
real guest captures and full-host qualification remain pending; issues stay open.

## Why

A pointer-only guest read does not establish the existing span-based block
contract. Closed constrained value-type calls exercise Info and ReadBlocks with
their original return types. No general BCL, filesystem or queue-reuse expansion
is included.

  `contract-efi-publish-diagnostic-r01.log`: CS0518 UnmanagedType and CS9064
  unsupported ref fields. After those minimal identities, r02 reports CS0117

  they are not wrapper/native acceptance. r03 and selected-profile r04 compile.

  or successful payload when _attempted/LastRead changes. Reject the entire span

  all 4096 bytes and invoke no device callbacks.

  overly broad symbol selection (two active allocation owners). The fixture now
  selects each owned routine and explicitly checks the two declared shared-read
  callers/arguments. r03 passes, including redirected-call rejection controls.
