## Files changed

- QemuTranscripts.SocketNetwork.cs extends existing lifetime validation only
  for explicit web controls (7 quota/8 normal lifetimes). Exact drop/timeout
  reports bind to two currently live peers and ordered network clocks. Timeout
  binds all nine handle/tuple fields to the current bad generation, requires no
  TIME-WAIT and at least30 seconds since creation, matching the configured
  close limit. A later original retire row is mandatory. Existing owner,
  generation reuse, private slot, TIME-WAIT/deadline and distinct-tuple guards
  remain. Historical5/6-lifetime controls retain their scope and reject M07 rows.
- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotWeb.Transport.cs exposes a value-only focused seam
  over that same validator and exact event-field decoding. Caller reports and
  strings do not establish descriptor, packet, scheduler or guest authority.
- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotSocket.cs consumes the exact two events without
  removing their scheduler positions. The bad accepted object's exact received/
  sent byte totals must match its raw exchange, and its fd must already be
  closed at timeout. The healthy accepted object must still have a live fd,
  no read/write shutdown/reset and exactly its first exchange at both events.
  The bad private retire row must subsequently be consumed. Retirement may
  precede or follow the healthy second request; only the bad lifetime is checked
  at that point. Final exact healthy I/O includes both complete exchanges.
  Transport observation clocks do not advance the released thread table.
- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotThreading.cs and NativeAotWeb.cs/Parent.cs carry
  explicit controls through the complete memory/syscall/FIFO/thread/wait/socket
  replay. Final success requires both events plus private retirement consumed,
  then sets TransportControlsChecked. Omitted controls retain historical scope
  and a false flag. Production checks the mandatory runner first and never
  selects that unchecked path.
- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotWeb.Packets.cs requires the runtime flag and passes
  the same controls through7/8 lifetimes and wire inspection. Every accepted
  connection and all sent/received bytes still join to the runtime totals, with
  original TIME-WAIT ports retained. Both wire and transport flags are reported
  as pending acceptance, subordinate to current source/native/runner authority.
- NativeAotWebTransportTests and NativeAotWebReplayTests add independent normal/
  quota vectors and mutations for reused generations, full identity, event
  order/clock/bounds, healthy overlap,30-second timeout, private fd ownership,
  exact I/O, later retirement and unchanged TIME-WAIT requirements. They use
  synthetic clocks/ELF/predicates and grant no guest execution authority.
- Scoped M10-007 owned paths and app-model/Kestrel docs record this boundary.
