## Evidence boundary

This acceptance covers a separate default-off, terminal QEMU diagnostic for
the exact modern fixture. The initial reset mapping assumes its declared
16 KiB BAR; actual sizing and installed page-table audit must succeed before
mapping acceptance. Numeric address binding alone is not mapping proof.
Failure callbacks and guest negative vectors do not prove real device faults
or device rejection of features. PCI/CPU access faults are outside the callback
contract. No DRIVER_OK, virtqueue publication, actual device DMA transfer,
interrupt handling, packet/block I/O, general hardware/IOMMU support, Gate 6,
hosted .NET/Kestrel, cloud support or production readiness is established.
