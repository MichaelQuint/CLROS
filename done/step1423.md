## Why

The continuing leaf could otherwise exceed syscall/time diagnostics while still
exhausting its finite startup random reservoir. Reusing exposed output after a
failed copy, sharing AT_RANDOM seeds or retaining firmware callbacks would break
the intended ownership boundary. This supplies the source/adapter foundation;
host and compile results do not qualify guest cryptographic behavior.
