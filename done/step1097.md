## Summary

Add and host-test the original C# predicates intended for #596's EFI selectors.
They check the compiler geometry measured in step1096, the expected active
exception state, exact clause ordering and the outer compiled continuation.

## Why

The second transfer must come from the actual inner catch, retain its original
handling context and exception, and search the outer method. A matching handler
address alone would not establish the frame, nesting, lifecycle or nonvolatile
state. These side-effect-free predicates permit exhaustive rejection checks
before wiring the real module/root/stack/control authorities.
