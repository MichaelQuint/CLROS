# Step1164: Bounded modern RX/header/ARP qualification

## Summary

Qualify #141–#144/#635: one default-off modern receive lifetime after the
accepted fixed TX parent, strict no-offload/single-buffer header, matching ARP
request/reply, caller-owned two-entry cache and seven supplied-tick lifecycle
controls. Implementation and failed development attempts remain in step1163.

## Why

Move one finite packet through the actual owned queue, validate it before cache
mutation and prove reset/bus-off/fence/reclamation and original allocator/command
restoration. This establishes bounded RX/ARP evidence without a continuing NIC
driver, timer service or broader protocol stack.
