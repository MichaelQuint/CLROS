## Summary

Implemented the #658 producer and #659 allocation-free lookup under owned

## Why

Existing adapters supply per-method code/EH sizes and individual EH getters.
The EH trailer discovers an address, but PE permissions, a root unwind row and
the parsed stream endpoint cannot establish exact compiler contribution sizes.
The new bounded catalog carries reviewed original compiler extents instead.
