## Summary

bytes and join reset versus normal-close resource totals. Remove the temporary
v2 launch block after installing the packet consumer; all existing original

## Why

FINs. Applying that policy to the v2 echo/stream/cancel protocol would either
reject its intended bytes or omit independent evidence. Cancellation resets
