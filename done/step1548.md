# Step 1548 - Preserve completion-before-Park in socket readers

## Summary

Socket readers now distinguish Parking3 from Pending5. The value-only reader
admits the actual completion-before-Park snapshot. The complete scheduler
replay still requires the exact state: Parking3 before Park, Ready1 after
publication. The directed-signal projection requires Parking3 for the pending
signal that wins immediately after repark. Kernel, app, timer and race ordering
are unchanged. No failed original is promoted by a repaired reader.

## Why

The readiness scan is committed and pushed at
`3e9e0000cc3fdc276d2a9ec94290b8ad4c20900a`. LAN branch and PR790 head readback
match, with a clean worktree. A fresh owned Smoke at that head reaches a real
repark followed by `completion=5 state=3`, then the reader rejects the valid
state with `socket_guest_wake_state`.

KernelThreadTable.State defines Ready1, Parking3 and Pending5. Complete keeps
Parking for a wake, deadline or interrupt that wins before Park; only Waiting
changes to Ready or Pending. Park later resumes an already-completed Parking
wait in place. The EFI producer faithfully records this snapshot. The old
reader incorrectly omitted3 and two downstream consumers mislabeled it5.
