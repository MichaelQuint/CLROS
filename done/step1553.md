## Summary

The fresh step1552 guest reaches the complete inner smoke success but correctly

## Why

The app's phase4 response is a scheduling hint. Waiter Phase3 is published
before entering the native recovery wait, so phase4 alone cannot order the
next HTTP request. The guest producer previously installed a completed-task
AfterAcknowledged hook, despite requiring native recovery-wait-before-overlap
in its final reader.

The original wire operation30/exchange15 is the 61-byte churn-overlap GET on
control connection1, bytes [913,974). That connection is native remote56641,
accepted fd7. Original nonpeek receives join that exact byte interval to raw
serial line26277, identity12884901898/ordinal300, before recovery epoll line26303,
identity12884901901/ordinal19/source3/sequence5/fd11/deadline0. The later route
dispatch log at26535 cannot replace actual byte consumption. Independent expert
review confirms a producer-order failure and preserves the strict predicate.
