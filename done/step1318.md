# Step 1318 - Complete bounded TCP close-codec transitions

## Summary

Complete the existing `TcpConnectionClose` transition prerequisite of #153.
The original five controls plus six new cases pass; the final TCP fast filter
passes 257 checks across 29 fixtures. This is host development within #734.

## Why

The existing active-close codec required a separate ACK before the peer FIN.
TCP may combine those events or close simultaneously. A repeated FIN must be
ACKed without consuming its sequence again. Reuse the existing status/packet
helpers and value-type staging rather than introduce another close framework.

## Files changed

- `src/OS.Kernel/Net/TcpConnectionClose.cs`: preserve all existing enum values,
  add Closing=8, admit a FIN+ACK in FinWait1, distinguish acknowledged local FIN
  from simultaneous close, and accept the later exact ACK in Closing.
  Regenerate ACK for a consumed FIN in CloseWait/LastAck/Closing/TimeWait,
  optionally applying its ACK of our FIN. Preserve sequence values and staged
  state on invalid inputs/short output. Nominal CompleteTimeWait changes only
  TimeWait to Closed; its caller must independently prove the real deadline.
- `tests/OS.HostTests/TcpConnectionCloseTests.cs`: six new cases for combined,
  simultaneous, repeated and wrapped FINs, ACK/output metadata, invalid flags/
  sequence/ACK/payload, short output, and stale events after nominal completion.
- Existing networking doc and M07-018 evidence only. Dependencies, labels and
  desired open state remain unchanged. No EFI/profile/table/buffer/timer edit.
