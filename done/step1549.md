## Summary

One stack-only ThreadRefresh admission now covers the source2 pipe scan and
following epoll refresh when no source2 waiter requires inspection. It retains
the exact descriptor and nonzero thread owner, consumes itself before refresh,
and falls back to full Attached admission when default, consumed or mismatched.
Every source2 Inspect/Wake discards it first. The full post-refresh epoll scan
and every retained-operation identity check remain.

Source and original trace review found one more exact repeated audit on the
actor's write path. WakePipeChanges first calls Attached, then scans source2

writes fd10 on another pipe. Its unchanged reader requires no Inspect/Wake,
callback or mutation. The tail epoll refresh repeated Attached over that same
unchanged graph. Admission is now shared only across those unchanged reads.
Changed source2 waits retain their original order, partial wakes and rejection
behavior and require fresh admission afterward. No audit is shared across
RefreshCore's readiness mutation.
