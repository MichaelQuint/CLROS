## Why

Earlier v8 request timing ended after host body-file Flush(true). It could not
separate response completion from file write/durable-flush cost. Also,
HttpClient.Timeout ends at headers with ResponseHeadersRead; subsequent stream
reads had only the remaining whole-peer token. Thus the stated 180-second body
budget was not separately enforced. See the official
[HttpCompletionOption remarks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.net.http.httpcompletionoption#remarks).
