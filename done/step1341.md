# Step 1341 - Linux socket packet dispatch and retained transport

## What changed and why

Add LinuxSocketNetwork under owned #737/#689, scoped/read back in comment21708.
The real socket guest needs a general packet dispatcher; the accepted TCP guest
selects scripted connections and deliberately uses tiny diagnostic buffers.

This owner borrows independently sized, zeroed caller storage and an empty owned
table. Exact IPv4 tuple lookup reconciles the retained generation with one passive
handshake, then genuine receive/send windows. Source MAC/IP, endpoint, IPv4/TCP
checksums and frame/storage extents are validated before tuple mutation. No fake
fd, packet, handshake, clock, socket readiness or remote peer is introduced.

Final ACK data is consumed once through the initialized stream before transfer.
Socket accept pressure retains established private windows; listener dup follows
the original description/inode, while close/reuse cannot redirect it. Pump drives
copied TX, ACK, retransmission, FIN, full TIME-WAIT and exact closed reap. Pending
attempt exhaustion stops retransmitting without abandoning the original deadline.
Reset remains retained until the last application fd closes. Explicit abort is
separate: detached threads and zero live fds are required; no graceful wire/RST
or normal TIME-WAIT completion is claimed by that path.

Socket/network metadata joins thread descriptor extent guards. Finish cannot
detach sockets until the network owner has drained and detached. Source3 EFI
worker completion/commit branches select CompleteEpollWait/CommitEpollWait only
under CLROS_LINUX_SOCKETS; existing profiles retain their prior path.

the exact peer wire sequence across reconnect, making the packet belong to the
new receive window despite an old ACK. Give the new handshake its own peer ISN,
verify the original table handle is stale, and verify old out-of-window payload
cannot enter the application. Its exact Ignored classification is checked after
the observed result; no TCP rule is weakened to satisfy the test.

global owner failure; retry limit wrongly treated as early timeout; aborting an

listener ownership and ignores late traffic to already closed retained entries.
