## Why and changes

The old fixtures read EfiEntry or a runtime owner from a single historical
filename and inferred authority from text fragments. HostTypeManagerSourceGuard
instead follows bound original-image capture, output locals, registration,
published-slot consumption and the registration guard before canary writes.
It checks the actual ReadWord/ReadAbi no-inline boundaries without imposing
a new attribute on Execute. Bounded-source checks inspect the selected owner,
including nested helpers, rather than unrelated types in a shared document.
Capture outputs require top-level writes before the sole successful return; assignments,
ref/out calls and ref aliases cannot replace the captured values or slot.

HostEfiSourceAdmission follows the existing family-pin/restricted-source policy.
M04 selects the admitted core family and bound compile-time authority. Its
runtime probe is checked semantically and compiled in the selected profile; it
never had a restricted-kernel pin. EH selects its kernel families and the bound
FrameCatcher, SimpleCatchSibling and FrameScratch sources, preserving all three
outputs of the original frame-dispatch file. No source/native pin was refreshed.
Unknown/unadmitted sources do not acquire authority through this helper.

EfiLeafReuseSources finds the actual owner across partials and binds admission,
decoder and walk calls. Four required constants are selected by their symbols,
independent of whether declarations are split or combined. The walk-caller
inventory includes nested helpers. Five existing behavioral leaf tests and the
forced-decoder oracle remain unchanged; source-order and missing-admission
controls use the rebound method's owning document. The integrated proposal also
moves an explanatory comment back to its relevant pool-audit test.

The shared IsUnmodifiedLocal helper now rejects ref-alias acquisition, closing
a mutation escape found during independent review. Runtime/kernel algorithms,
ABI contracts, resource limits and guest diagnostics are unchanged in this slice.
