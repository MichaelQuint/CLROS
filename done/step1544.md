# Step 1544 - Consolidate repeated descriptor audits within serialized calls

## Summary

Remove three immediately repeated, read-only descriptor audits without caching
validation across operations or dropping any distinct admission predicate.
The complete descriptor audit includes the Linux memory map and socket/epoll
ownership graph. Rechecking that unchanged graph made every syscall more costly.

## Why

The original failed prewriteyield Smoke remains failed. Its preserved actor
timings show12.4168ms for clock228 and27.6129ms for write1 before timer preemption.
These aggregate stages do not identify an individual function's contribution.
Source review identifies exact duplicate audits with no intervening mutation or
callback. This is a general synchronous-path optimization, not a scheduler or
acceptance change. The application, package, timer, required race order, single
attempt and all cancellation/cleanup requirements remain unchanged.

  mappings and changed thread/epoll identity before user copy or readiness

- `docs/kestrel.md` and this reserved step record the development boundary.
