# Step 1438 - Recheck queued signals before new interruptible waits

## Summary

Development-only first slice of M16-020/#760 in the five-ticket repeated-service
and churn batch under #737. A real host schedule reproduces stolen pipe

## Why

An earlier Woken completion correctly wins over a later signal. Once another
worker consumes that readiness, retry previously staged a fresh wait without
checking the still-queued signal. Preserve the old winner while interrupting
the replacement through the existing completion-before-Park path.
