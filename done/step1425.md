The existing socket write policy accepts an explicit `requireSpace` value,
defaulting to the previous behavior. Sendmsg supplies `total !=0`; scalar
sendto supplies `bytes !=0`. Empty sends retain metadata/flag/connected/reset/
timeout/write-shutdown and unsupported blocking-mode policy, then return0
without send-space refusal, payload copy, transport queue or yielded EAGAIN.
Nonempty paths retain the original capacity and partial-send behavior.

## Why

The original review's two host cases return-EAGAIN for empty sendmsg when an
otherwise live send window is full. Neither empty operation needs send space.
Do not bypass endpoint errors or unsupported flags with blanket early success.
Scalar sendto shares the same policy and bug. Descriptor write/writev already
return zero under their existing zero-I/O ordering; they were inspected and
left unchanged rather than importing sendmsg ordering into other APIs.
