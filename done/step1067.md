## What changed and why

firmware-exit continuation. The BootSequence-gated operation performs one
allocate/write/read/restore/free cycle with original accounting and emits the
owned diagnostic reporter once. It then admits all original ACPI workspace,
copy, region and scan spans before post-exit readback. Firmware physical
addresses remain identities; the code consumes copied offsets and snapshots.
Original descriptor storage, native adoption observations and canaries are
validated without claiming a fresh SGDT/SIDT/STR observation.

panic path. The latter loads the original unmapped guard through a non-inlined
C# method, validates original vector14/error0/CR2/CR3 and IST2 frame authority in
the installed callback, then uses the unchanged owned terminal reporter.
The runner's distinct expected-panic mode preserves the measured quiet drain,
owned teardown and exit19 boundary. Positive and panic grammars are 76/77
application records and 78/79 stream records respectively. No guest execution
is claimed by implementing these paths.

The original allocator core and fixture from step1065 remain byte-identical.
Five canonical docs now record the development behavior and limitations.
