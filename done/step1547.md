# Step 1547 - Bounded retained epoll readiness scan

## Summary

One stack-only readiness scan can share a successful descriptor-graph audit
across consecutive not-ready retained epoll operations. The first operation
and each changed owner still take the full post-refresh audit. Every visit
checks Active, thread owner, description bounds, exact Self, epoll kind,
inode, port and retained borrower. Ordinary HasEvents retains full admission.

The caller discards the scan before any thread-table Inspect or Wake. It keeps
the original slot order and immediate wake behavior, including an earlier
successful wake when a later operation is rejected. No audit survives a
callback, mutation, syscall or later poll. There are no new allocations,
public unchecked entry points, timer changes or application changes.

## Why

The preceding original at `8b2d6a38bd0a5bce347c6bc913a811b04b535325` still
failed the required signal-race recovery gate. The actor completed its clock,
write and directed signal before timer preemption, but the waiter returned
readiness before the actor consumed the byte. Original timing intervals are
8.2578ms clock,14.7010ms write,7.0886ms tgkill and12.3623ms later read.
They exclude initial owner validation, logging and native return; they do not
attribute one function's cost or guarantee a different schedule.

That original trace has two retained epoll waits before the actor's clock:
Kestrel identity4294967302/sequence35 and race waiter12884901900/sequence1.
Neither completes before that call. Source review found each not-ready wait
repeated the same full graph audit after RefreshThreads without intervening
mutation or callback. This change removes that duplicate synchronous work
while retaining the post-refresh audit and each operation's exact guards.
