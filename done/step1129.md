## Evidence

The guest selects exactly synthetic 1b36:0005 at 00:06.0. Firmware Command=7 is
recorded before the adapter clears bus mastering with a WORD write and proves
Command=3. Both BAR probes restore 3; the terminal diagnostic never re-enables
bus mastering. The general probe rejects enabled bus mastering before writes.
BAR0 is nonprefetchable memory32 at 0x81041000, length 4096. BAR2 is prefetchable
memory64 at 0xC000000000, length 0x200000; BAR3 is its upper slot. Only this
function's ECAM page is writable UC/NX; adjacent pages remain read-only.
No BAR payload is accessed. QEMU's dedicated test device behavior is documented
at https://www.qemu.org/docs/master/specs/pci-testdev.html.

One page from the original owned allocator is admitted as explicit identity
CPU/physical/device correspondence. Its first and last words are written and
restored. Pool exhaustion, in-flight release, stale generation and double release
are rejected. The exact backing receipt is returned: free pages 2 -> 1 -> 2,
allocated pages 0 after cleanup. A separate nonidentity numeric vector exercises
address-domain preservation; it is not an IOMMU mapping or device transaction.
