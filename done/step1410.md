## Files changed

- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotWeb.Controls.cs strictly decodes bounded JSON. Exact
  object/property inventories reject duplicates, extras, missing/incorrect
  types and noncanonical/overflowed numbers. Ports, exact loopback redirector
  arguments, forwarded/withheld budgets, increasing original ordinals, one VLAN
  and healthy joined shutdown are checked. Three request/response pairs require
  independent literal requests, exact17-byte health, close/keep-alive headers,
  canonical Date, bounded UTC/elapsed intervals and healthy Date advance.
  Timeout/drop rows must have the exact isolated tuple/clock/device fields.
- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotWeb.Runner.cs requires these reports by default,
  checks all9 normal/8 quota UTC windows inside the original owned child, joins
  the last3 exactly to the control exchanges and binds the unique ordered event
  rows to original serial. Ordinary stdout/probe rules stay exact. Explicit
  requireControls=false is historical synthetic inspection only, returns
  Controls=null and is never selected by production.
- QemuTranscripts.NativeAotWeb.Packets.cs supports8 normal/7 quota connections
  when controls are supplied. The bad closing response needs no FIN because
  withheld ACKs prevent drain. The last connection contains independent exact
  keep-alive and closing requests plus two complete responses. Exactly one
  literal1518-byte VLAN occurs after complete first healthy response and before
  its second request. Other jumbo/VLAN traffic rejects. Every withheld frame
  binds valid MAC/IP/TCP/checksum, bad tuple, no payload, control flags and
  bounded sequence/ACK; at least one acknowledges the whole bad response.
  Those ACKs must be absent from the guest-side capture. Raw HTTP/Date/UTC joins,
  retransmission equality and complete byte accounting remain mandatory.
  ForwardedFrames remains ancillary host-writer accounting: successful framed
  writes prove neither QEMU delivery nor capture completion, and mixed-direction
  PCAP has no authenticated backend-direction identifier. No exact count join
  is claimed. ControlsChecked describes the explicitly listed wire joins only.
- NativeAotWebRunnerTests/NativeAotWebPacketTests add independent positive and
  rejection cases for both normal/quota shapes, exact schemas/lifetimes, ports,
  bounds, original events, raw HTTP, UTC, VLAN order/bytes and withheld traffic.
  Historical small fixtures explicitly retain their unchecked evidence scope.
- M10-007 ownedPaths, docs/kestrel.md and docs/app-model.md record the scope.
