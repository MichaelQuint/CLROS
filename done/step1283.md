## Why

Raw output and packaging alone cannot qualify runtime execution. The focused
consumer still assumed <=8 workers/32 entropy bytes and did not model syscall219.
Broader output must agree with actual modeled signal frames, deadline completions
and worker reaps, including segmentation/coalescing and generation reuse.

and return success before the next active-step check. Sample genuine final time
after reap, retain it before native metadata scrub and validate it with start.
