## Summary

Add the exclusive retained Virtio-net device owner for #691/#734. Ten focused
owner checks, nine ring checks and four existing queue checks pass. This is

## Why

The ring engine alone cannot configure or notify a device, bound outstanding TX
with a real clock callback, or return DMA safely. Reuse the accepted queue helper
and existing page-aligned pool rather than duplicating register setup or ownership.

  Stop requires a fresh reset/readback, bus-master disable and fence before scrub,
