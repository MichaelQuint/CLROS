# Step 1038 - Seeded churn transcript validator preparation

## What changed and why

Add the small record validator needed to connect #83's implemented guest probe to
the smoke runner. Reuse the existing raw transcript and GC/heap checks rather than
duplicating them. Preparation ownership is recorded in issue83 comment15709; the
running collector checkout and canonical integration files remain frozen.

The helper requires eleven ordered M04_CHURN records plus CLROS_M04_014_READY.
It binds the type/static root/child to the preceding validated collector records,
checks the three seeds' exact rounds, allocations/reclamations, collection/reuse
counts and fingerprints, then verifies cumulative allocation identities, retained
payload, aligned nonincreasing stack witnesses within the original range, and
the original canaries. Arithmetic on cumulative identities uses decimal to reject
UInt64 overflow. Numeric records require exact uppercase 16-digit hex fields.
