# Step 1764 - Evaluate explicit provider contracts and compiled owner prefixes

## Summary

Replace manual per-caller arithmetic with a host-only explicit contract evaluator.
Keep incomplete roots unbounded and propagate their missing providers through
named targets. Independent pool/descriptor reviews corroborate their stated
static slices. Extend the compiled-layout probe with integer enum widths and
explicit field-prefix selections; do not infer unsupported fixed-buffer tails.

## Why

Reviewing isolated callback numbers is insufficient to bound every caller.
Duplicated tables can also assign a bound to the wrong target. The evaluator
derives named target costs from their expressions and identifies exactly what
remains unknown. The previous byte/uint-only enum rule unnecessarily prevented
ordinary int-enum owner layouts; prefix selection avoids requiring unrelated
unsupported tail fields to establish a consumed field's offset.
