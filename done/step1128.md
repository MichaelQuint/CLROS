## Implementation boundary

BAR probing snapshots all six type-0 slots and Command. It rejects bus mastering,
unsupported headers/types and invalid output storage. Decode is disabled with a
16-bit Command write, preserving W1C Status. A 64-bit BAR consumes its upper
slot. Every path after the first attempted write attempts all restorations;
readbacks gate restoring Command. Failed restore reports uncertainty and tries
to keep decode disabled. Exclusive quiescent ownership, valid UC mappings and
non-throwing callbacks are adapter obligations. CPU faults/hotplug are unsupported.
The guest fixture probes and repeats QEMU pci-testdev at 00:06.0, including a
4 KiB memory32 BAR and 2 MiB prefetchable memory64 BAR. It does not touch BAR payloads.

DMA windows carry distinct virtual/physical/device addresses and enforce aligned
nonwrapping ranges and device width. Caller-owned pools cover at most 64 pages,
allocate contiguous runs, bind opaque tokens to pool/epoch/slot/generation,
retire exhausted generations, and reject stale, foreign, double and in-flight
release. CPU/device transitions are bookkeeping, not hardware synchronization.
The guest adapter allocates one page from the original allocator, explicitly
uses identity address correspondence on declared QEMU/no-IOMMU hardware, writes
and restores two words, exercises pool ownership/reuse, and frees the exact
backing receipt. Independent nonidentity vectors are numeric contracts only.
