# Step 1447 - Bound fd-less unread TCP retirement

## Summary

M07-034/#778 development under #737 repairs bulk-slot retention after the last
fd makes unread data inaccessible. Signal loss using an exact-sequence RST|ACK;
retain ownership through copied-TX pressure, then closed reap scrubs before
release. All54 focused network/close/frame checks pass. Native/guest acceptance
and stable full-host qualification remain pending;778 stays open/claimed.

## Why

Unread RX prevented compact TIME-WAIT transfer while no fd remained to drain it.
One byte could pin the sole bulk slot indefinitely. Apply the reset recommendation
for pending or later data after CLOSE in RFC9293 section3.6.1, without clearing
RX for a readable duplicate or SHUT_WR alone.
