# Step 1469 - Configured service memory-operation accounting

## What and why

Reuse the existing post-dispatch memory snapshot for every configured-v2 memory
Return, so deliberate native pressure can be distinguished from ordinary churn.
The retained lifetime peak alone cannot measure ordinary headroom. Legacy v1
and finite profiles retain failure-only output. No allocator or capacity changes.

The strict copied-value reader requires exact bounded snapshots adjacent to
matching returned calls, complete operation coverage and lifetime peak bounds.
UTF8 byte lengths bind split stderr markers to original writes. Each native
pressure window requires its probe-shaped ENOMEM and unchanged owner state.
A physical shortfall is reported only when free pages are fewer than requested;
it does not identify the sole failing guard. Other failures remain unclassified.
Ordinary backing includes its starting backing and excludes deliberate pressure.
The conservative pool/entitlement estimate treats that entire peak as non-GC,
so it may overestimate demand. A failed estimate is reported, not accepted.
