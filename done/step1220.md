## Why

The existing bounded MSI-X wait handler saves onlyRAX and depends on the wait's
R15 context. It cannot preempt an arbitrary non-yielding mechanical context.
The new entry saves all fifteen explicit GPRs before scratch; hardware preserves
the interrupted RSP in the five-word no-error frame, even for CPL0/ownedIST.
FS/GS bases and aligned x87/SSE state precede neutral-FP restoration/C# callback.
The saved hardware flags remain separate from callback-entry flags.

Win64 callback receives(context, frame) with aligned CALL RSP/32-byte shadow.
The live owner must freshly admit the complete frame and return exactlybyte1.
CLI precedes restoration; complete/CS8/SS16 and both restored-base readbacks are
checked. All GPRs/FP are restored before IRETQ pops RIP/CS/RFLAGS/oldRSP/SS.
Failure reaches an IF-clear retaining loop, never a fabricated successful return.
